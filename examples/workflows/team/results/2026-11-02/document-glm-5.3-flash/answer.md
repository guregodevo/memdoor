Here's the short version of pull requests, which you'll see every day.

A pull request is a way of saying: "here are changes I made on a branch — please look at them before they become part of our shared work." A branch is your own private copy, where you build and test safely without touching what everyone relies on.

Why we use them: nobody's code goes in unseen. A teammate reads your changes while they're still easy to fix, catches mistakes early on, and learns what you've done. It also keeps our main version trustworthy, so anyone can build on it without fear. And it's how knowledge travels: someone else understands your change too.

It's not about formality — it's how we look out for each other. Questions are welcome anytime — I'm happy to sit with you through your very first one!
