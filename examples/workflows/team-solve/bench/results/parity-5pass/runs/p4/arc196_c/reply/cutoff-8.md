The attempt was pursuing a hybrid algorithm: extract event pairs from the string, solve small cases with an O(M²) DP, and solve large cases with a modular NTT/convolution-based method. It had implemented event extraction, root precomputation, the direct solver, and most of the large-solver NTT setup, including a forward NTT. It stopped while writing the inverse NTT, before completing the convolution, coefficient updates, final answer computation, or any benchmark harness. As a result, it did not yet produce timed N=200000 results for alternating, long-run, or random strings, nor identify or fix performance bottlenecks.
