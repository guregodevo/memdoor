The attempt was pursuing an NTT/CDQ-style large-solver implementation with `solve_direct` as an O(m²) reference, plus constants intended to force the large path by keeping `DIRECT_M` small. It established reusable infrastructure: modular NTT, generic convolution, `convolve_fact`, `poly_inv`/`make_P`, and a direct solver that builds `H` and the answer by summing over nonzero previous indices. It got as far as starting `solve_large`’s recursive contribution routine `add_contrib(l, mid, r)`, where it collects nonzero left-half `H` values, uses direct loops for small `cnt * right_len` or single-source cases, and begins setting up a convolution by computing `t_min`, `t_max`, `k_min`, and `k_max`. It stopped before completing the convolution branch, the outer recursive driver, the random/structured stress tests for N=3000–10000, the timed N=200000 benchmarks, and any correctness or performance fixes.
